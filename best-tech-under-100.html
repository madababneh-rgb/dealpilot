Best Tech Under $100: A Smarter Way to Compare

Shopping for tech under $100 is often less about finding one perfect product and more about avoiding compatibility problems and paying for features you will never use. ### Build your shortlist

Start with the main job: charging, audio, computer accessories, smart-home use, travel or entertainment. Then choose three products that meet the basic requirements.

Charging accessories

Check charging standards, wattage, port types and included cables. A compact charger can be more useful than a larger unit if you travel frequently.

Headphones and audio

Compare battery life, microphone quality, comfort, controls and compatibility. If you will use them for calls, do not rely only on music reviews.

Computer accessories

Keyboards, mice, webcams, hubs and stands can improve a workspace without requiring a major upgrade. Check operating-system compatibility and available ports.

Smart-home products

Before buying, confirm whether a device works with the ecosystem you already use. A low price is not a bargain if the device requires equipment you do not have.

Our quick comparison rule

Separate must-have features from nice-to-have features. If two products both satisfy every must-have, then price, warranty, reviews and convenience can break the tie.
