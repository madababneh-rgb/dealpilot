Product Comparison Checklist: 10 Questions Before You Buy

Comparing products becomes easier when you use the same questions every time. This checklist is designed to keep the focus on value instead of marketing language.
  1. What problem does the product solve?
  2. What are the two or three must-have features?
  3. Does it fit my exact situation?
  4. What is included?
  5. Will I need extra accessories?
  6. What do recent reviews say?
  7. Are there repeated complaints?
  8. What is the return policy?
  9. What is the total cost?
  10. Would I buy it without the sale label?

Don't let star ratings do all the work

A rating is a useful starting point, but it does not tell you whether a product is right for your specific use. A product can be highly rated and still be wrong for your needs.

Compare like with like

When possible, compare products with similar size, capacity, power, included accessories and warranty. Otherwise a price comparison can be misleading.

Keep your shortlist small

Three strong candidates are usually easier to evaluate than twenty. Once you have three, stop searching unless you discover a missing requirement.
